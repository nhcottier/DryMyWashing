Dry My Washing

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Dry My Washing helps you decide whether conditions are suitable for drying washing outdoors. This privacy policy explains what information the app uses and how that information is handled.

Information the app uses

  • Precise location, if you allow location access on your device
  • Approximate location based on your IP address if location access is unavailable
  • Search text you enter when looking up a place
  • An Apple-generated app transaction identifier, original app version, and App Store environment to provide one non-renewing 14-day trial

How the information is used

  • Find your location or the place you searched for
  • Request weather and forecast data relevant to that location
  • Show you drying guidance inside the app
  • Start the 14-day trial once, check its expiry, and prevent the same Apple account from restarting it by reinstalling the app

Data sharing with third parties

To provide weather and location lookups, the app sends location-related requests to services that support the app’s functionality. These services may process your request data according to their own terms and privacy policies. The iPhone app uses Apple Weather through WeatherKit for forecast data.

  • Apple Weather through WeatherKit for weather forecast data in the iPhone app
  • Apple Maps and geocoding services for place search and current-location names in the iPhone app
  • ipapi for approximate IP-based location lookup
  • Cloudflare hosts the Dry My Washing trial service that verifies Apple-signed app transaction information

Data storage

Dry My Washing does not require you to create an account. The app does not sell your personal information. Based on the current app design, location lookups and forecast requests are used to deliver results in the app and are not intended to build a personal profile about you.

The trial service stores the Apple-generated app transaction identifier, the original app version, App Store environment, and trial start, expiry, and last-verification times. It does not receive your location, weather searches, name, email address, or forecast data.

Your choices

You can deny location access in iOS Settings and still use the app by searching for a place manually. You can also stop using the app at any time.

Contact
